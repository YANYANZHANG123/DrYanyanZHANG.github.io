👨‍🔬 About Me 关于我

I am Yanyan Zhang, a researcher working on Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and spaceborne remote sensing. My research focuses on advanced radar architectures and signal processing methods for high-resolution, wide-swath, and high-precision Earth observation.

My work covers bistatic and distributed SAR, MIMO-SAR, SAR interferometry, high-resolution wide-swath (HRWS) SAR, SAR synchronization, interferometric baseline estimation, and DEM generation. I am particularly interested in future multi-satellite SAR systems and the signal-processing challenges introduced by large bistatic angles, long baselines, multi-channel architectures, and wide-swath operation.
